Computer Science Education

Making programming accessible to everyone.

I am Florian Wörister, Lecturer/Researcher at the Faculty of Computer Science and Applied Mathematics at the University of Applied Sciences Technikum Wien and an external lecturer at the University of Vienna, specializing in programming education. With a background in computer science, music education and certification as a Carpentries instructor, my research focuses on innovative methods to make programming concepts accessible to learners of all backgrounds and ages.
